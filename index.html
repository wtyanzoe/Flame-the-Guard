Rules notes

Game modes: choose "Play with a friend" for pass-and-play on one device, or "Play alone" to face an AI opponent as Player 2.

Deck size: 80 cards (50 main + 30 protection). Built from the listed frequencies in MAIN_DEFS/PROT_DEFS. Fire barriers are tracked separately and are not counted as deck cards.

Positions: 5 positions. Each player has a lane at each position and may stack up to 2 main cards per lane — so 2×5 = 10 slots per player.

First player: Player 1 places first on odd rounds, Player 2 on even rounds.

Pass-and-play placement: both hands hidden until a player taps "Reveal my hand". Each player places face-down, then hands device over. "Flip cards" reveals both placements.

AI mode: P2's hand is always hidden. The AI auto-places, draws and attaches protections, burns your protections with Arsonists, and passes on swapping.

Round 1: Draw → Place → Flip → Face-off only. Scientist / Construction worker / Arsonist abilities do not activate. No protection, no arsonist removal, no swapping.

Round 2+: Draw → Place → Flip → Protection → Arsonist removal → Swapping → Face-off.

Protection draw: every Construction worker on the board adds +1 protection card; every Scientist on the board adds +2 protection cards. All of a player's protection draws for the round happen at once during their protection phase. A card placed in round N (and still on the board) contributes in round N and every round thereafter — including the round it was placed. If a Construction worker or Scientist leaves the board, it stops contributing.

Protection placement: select a protection card from your hand, then click a target non-Fire card — it attaches immediately. Click Done when finished.

Binding peptide: CsgA and BslA require a Binding peptide on that specific main card first. Once a peptide is on a card, any number of CsgA/BslA may be attached using that glue.

Protection card disposition: a protection card is attached to a specific main card. Whenever that main card leaves the board — destroyed by face-off, discarded by swap, or any other means — its protection cards are discarded with it. They never return to the protection hand.

Arsonist: each Arsonist on your board lets you remove 1 opponent protection card per round. Inactive in round 1. 🔥 Burn buttons appear in the action bar for each burnable protection card. Selections are staged — click Done to apply all of them at once. If you burn a Binding peptide, and that was the last peptide on the card, all CsgA and BslA attached to that card are also discarded (still costs only 1 removal).

Swap: select board card(s) in one column, then hand card(s); incoming total currency (💰) must be ≤ outgoing total, and the column may not exceed 2 main cards. Cards swapped off the board are discarded — along with any protection cards attached to them.

Face-off: triggered when both players have at least one card in the same position. Stacked cards pool their ⚔ ATK and 🛡 RES; protection cards add to 🛡 RES.

Combat (simultaneous): both directions resolve from the pre-combat state, comparing the attacker's ⚔ ATK against the defender's 🛡 RES:
• ⚔ ATK > 🛡 RES → defender destroyed; defender loses (⚔ ATK − 🛡 RES) fire barriers.
• ⚔ ATK = 🛡 RES → defender destroyed; 0 fire barriers lost.
• ⚔ ATK < 🛡 RES → absorbed; nothing happens.
Each contested position is reviewed with an OK — Resolve button.

Game end — fire barriers depleted: a player who loses all 10 fire barriers loses immediately; the other player wins.

Game end — deck exhaustion: if the main deck cannot supply enough cards for both players to draw to 5 at the start of a round, or if the protection deck cannot supply enough cards for both players' combined protection draws in a round, the game ends immediately at that point. The player with more fire barriers remaining wins. If barriers are tied, the game is a draw.

🔍 State: click the header button at any time to dump full game state to the log.
