🔥 Flame the Guard/Guard the Flame

v2
Rules notes & interpretation choices

Deck size: the listed frequencies total 80 cards (50 main + 30 protection), not 105. The deck is built from the listed frequencies — edit MAIN_DEFS/PROT_DEFS in the script to reach 105.

Positions: 5 positions. Each player has a lane at each position and may stack up to 2 main cards per lane — so 2×5 = 10 slots per player.

First player: No RPS. Player 1 places first on odd rounds, Player 2 on even rounds.

Pass-and-play placement: both hands hidden until a player taps "Reveal my hand". Each player places face-down, then hands device over. "Flip cards" reveals both placements.

Round 1: Draw → Place → Flip → Face-off only. Scientist / Construction worker / Arsonist abilities do not activate. No protection, no arsonist removal, no swapping.

Round 2+: Draw → Place → Flip → Protection → Arsonist removal → Swapping → Face-off.

Protection draw: every Construction worker on the board gives +1; every Scientist on the board gives +2. Draws accumulate across rounds. A Scientist placed in round N contributes in round N+1 onward, as long as it remains on the board. Only drawn once per round per player. If a Scientist is destroyed or swapped away, it stops contributing.

Protection card disposition: a protection card is attached to a specific main card. Whenever that main card leaves the board — destroyed by face-off, discarded by swap, or any other means — its protection cards are discarded with it. They never return to the protection hand.

Face-off: triggered when both players have at least one card in the same position. Stacked cards pool ATK and RES; protection cards add to RES.

Combat (simultaneous): both directions resolve from the pre-combat state. ATK > RES → destroyed + (ATK−RES) fire barriers lost. ATK = RES → destroyed, no barriers lost. ATK < RES → absorbed. Each contested position is reviewed with an OK — Resolve button.

Binding peptide: CsgA and BslA require a Binding peptide on that specific main card first. Once a peptide is on a card, any number of CsgA/BslA may be attached using that glue. A different card needs its own peptide.

Arsonist: each Arsonist on your board lets you remove 1 opponent protection card per round. Inactive in round 1. 🔥 Burn buttons appear in the action bar for each burnable protection card. If you burn a Binding peptide, and that was the last peptide on the card, all CsgA and BslA attached to that card are also discarded (still costs only 1 removal). Burning a CsgA or BslA on its own does not remove the peptide.

Swap: select board card(s) in one column, then hand card(s); incoming total currency must be ≤ outgoing total, and the column may not exceed 2 main cards. Cards swapped off the board are discarded — along with any protection cards attached to them.

Game end — fire barriers depleted: a player who loses all 10 fire barriers loses immediately; the other player wins.

Game end — deck exhaustion: if the main deck cannot supply enough cards for both players to draw to 5 at the start of a round, or if the protection deck cannot supply enough cards for both players' combined protection draws in a round, the game ends immediately at that point (the round does not complete). The player with more fire barriers remaining wins. If barriers are tied, the game is a draw.

Example: in Round 10, Player 1 has 2 Construction workers (2 protection draws) and Player 2 has 1 Scientist (2 draws). Total needed for the round = 4. If the protection deck has fewer than 4 cards, the game ends in Round 10 before either player draws. The player with more fire barriers remaining wins; tie → draw.

🔍 State: click the header button at any time to dump full game state to the log.
